Face Analyser project

OVERSIGHT.SYS — Facial Recognition & Analyzer OVERSIGHT.SYS is a camera monitoring application that analyzes live video, detects and recognizes enrolled faces, and records events for review. It supports computer and phone cameras, publicly reachable RTSP streams, and local-network cameras through a relay agent. Technologies: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, face-api.js, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM, Google Cloud Storage, FFmpeg, Python with OpenCV, and browser camera APIs. Features include: Live webcam, RTSP, and relay-based camera feeds Face enrollment, detection, and matching against saved profiles Face landmarks, expression analysis, and estimated age and gender Unknown-person detection and access-control alerts Event history, alerts, analytics, and report generation Face-triggered video recordings with saved playback Camera registration and a PIN-protected app screen
